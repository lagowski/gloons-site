AI tools, tested and explained.

Gloons is a Spanish-language channel that tries AI tools, explains AI concepts so anyone can understand them, and publishes what it finds on X, the @GloonsAI YouTube channel, Instagram and Facebook.

What the Gloons app does

The Gloons app is an internal publishing tool used only by the Gloons team. It lets us:

  • upload our own finished videos, with their titles and descriptions, to our own YouTube channel, after a team member has approved each one;
  • read our own channel's information to check that uploads worked;
  • create and update, in our own Google Drive, the files the app itself produces (the resource guides we share with our followers).

The app does not offer accounts to the public and does not access anyone else's data.
